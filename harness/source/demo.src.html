How this harness is built

  • Open: MIT code, CC-BY-4.0 data, zero dependencies. Public goods (misconception sets, skills) are loaded through a registry that only accepts open licenses.
  • Model-agnostic: swap the reference model for any OpenAI-compatible model, including open-weight models.
  • Context you can see: each proposal shows exactly what was put in the model's context, what was dropped for budget, and why.
  • Grounded in learning science: misconceptions, productive struggle, zone of proximal development, cognitive load and teacher agency.
  • Human in the loop: nothing is applied until a teacher approves, edits or rejects it.
  • Privacy by design: pseudonymous ids only, names are rejected, emails and phone numbers are masked, and nothing is stored on a server.
  • Fairness check: a plain table of support levels by group, so a teacher can notice patterns.
